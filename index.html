The crew

Two passports,
one shared window seat.

We're Ciejay and Satiko — a couple who started dating over cheap seat sales and questionable jeepney directions. Somewhere between the third missed ferry and the first free upgrade, we started keeping notes, so the stories wouldn't turn into "you had to be there." This is that notebook, made public.

Expect real budgets, real arguments about aircon, and a lot of white-sand beaches we'll probably never stop writing about.
